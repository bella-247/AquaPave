Profile

Role in AquaPave

Fikir leads the architectural design: the wash-pad layout, 2–5% slope, 2D/3D drawings, and scale model that show how pavement, filter, and tank fit together.

The role coordinates dimensions with civil and electromechanical engineering, and checks that the pad can be cleaned and used by a small garage.

Key deliverables

  • Wash-pad plan, section, and layout drawings.
  • 2–5% slope design and drainage path.
  • 3D views of the integrated prototype.
  • Scale model for reviews and the final demonstration.
