Profile

Role in AquaPave

Betelhem leads sensor testing and calibration: comparing readings with reference instruments, troubleshooting connections, and verifying that the data can be trusted.

The role supports the electrical and software teams during integration, and keeps a record of errors, fixes, and acceptance checks.

Key deliverables

  • Calibration procedure and reference test points.
  • Sensor accuracy and drift records.
  • Troubleshooting log for wiring and readings.
  • Final verification summary for the report.
