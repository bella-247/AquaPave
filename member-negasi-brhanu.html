Profile

Role in AquaPave

Negasi leads sensor selection, signal conditioning, and power: choosing the water-level and turbidity sensors, designing the circuits, and setting up a reliable supply.

The role works with Helen Kinfu on wiring and with Betelhem on calibration so the readings are stable and accurate.

Key deliverables

  • Water-level and turbidity sensor selection.
  • Signal-conditioning circuits and wiring interface.
  • Electrical or solar power supply plan.
  • Calibration support and electrical test records.
