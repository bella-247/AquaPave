Profile

Role in AquaPave

Natnael leads the environmental engineering work: characterising garage wastewater, preparing the synthetic wash water, and checking the treated water against non-potable reuse limits.

The role sets the sampling schedule used by the civil and chemical teams, and connects the lab results back to the project's water-saving and pollution goals.

Key deliverables

  • Addis Ababa garage wastewater profile.
  • Synthetic wash-water recipe and preparation procedure.
  • Stage-wise sampling plan and quality-control checks.
  • Non-potable reuse assessment and daily water-saving estimate.
