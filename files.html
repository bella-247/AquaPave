Section 01

Problem & Governing Equations

Formulates the unmanaged discharge of garage wash water into Addis Ababa streams. Defines Darcy's Law infiltration (Q = k · i · A) and Stokes' Law droplet separation (vs) sizing the oil trap to ≥ 0.04 m².

Section 02

Multidisciplinary WBS & Schedule

Structures responsibilities across 7 disciplines (Civil, Chemical, Environmental, Architecture, Electromechanical, Electrical & Computer, Software), establishing 6 gate reviews and interface contracts.

Section 03

Wastewater & Media Specifications

Benchmarks motor oil, surfactants, and heavy metals. Details ACI 522R-10 pervious concrete (15%–35% void ratio, w/c 0.27–0.34) and a 3-layer stratified sand-carbon-gravel adsorption filter bed.

Section 04

SDG Alignment & Traceability

Maps technical performance targets to UN SDGs: primary alignment with SDG 6 (Clean Water), SDG 9 (Infrastructure), and SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities), with supporting impacts on SDG 1 and SDG 7.
