LAYER 01 · SURFACE WASH APRON ACI 522R-10 / ASTM C1701

Pervious Concrete Slab

Single-size 4.75–9.5 mm crushed basalt aggregate bonded with Portland cement paste (w/c = 0.36) with sand eliminated. Captures wash runoff instantaneously to eliminate surface ponding and initiates coarse particulate trapping.

1
Pore Matrix (15%–35% Voids) Interconnected pore channels provide rapid hydraulic permeability exceeding ASTM C1701 benchmarks.
2
Alkaline Buffering (pH 8.0–9.5) Hydrated cement phases chemically neutralize mildly acidic wash waters and assist heavy metal precipitation.
Thickness 80 mm Depth
Compressive Strength 14.8 MPa (ASTM C39)
Target Void Ratio 20.4% Bulk Volume
Transverse Slope 2.5% Pitch to Edge
LAYER 02 · BEDDING & FILTRATION ASTM D4751 / D4833

Choker Stone & Geotextile Membrane

Stabilizes the pervious slab above while preventing sand migration from below. The non-woven geotextile provides critical fines retention without impeding gravity vertical drainage.

1
5–10 mm Choker & Bedding Stone Uniform aggregate layer distributes vehicular wheel load and establishes a uniform hydraulic gradient.
2
Non-Woven Geotextile Membrane High-permittivity (>0.5 s−¹) synthetic needle-punched fabric separates coarse and fine media zones.
Thickness 50 mm Bedding
Fabric Weight 200 g/m² Polypropylene
Apparent Opening (AOS) 0.15 mm (ASTM D4751)
Puncture Strength >1.8 kN (ASTM D4833)
LAYER 03 · FINE SUSPENDED SOLIDS ASTM C33 SPECIFICATION

Washed River Sand Bed

Washed silica river sand removes fine suspended particles through physical interstitial straining and depth sedimentation, shielding the carbon bed below from premature particulate blinding.

1
Graded Washed Silica Sand Matrix Clean quartz sand with silt content <1% provides controlled filtration without cementation.
2
Anti-Blinding Pre-Filter Barrier Arrests 186–561 mg/L influent TSS to protect downstream high-value adsorbent media.
Thickness 60 mm Layer
Grain Size Range 0.5–2.0 mm (Washed)
Uniformity Coeff. (Cu) <2.5 (Uniform Sizing)
TSS Retention >65% Single Pass
LAYER 04 · ORGANIC & HYDROCARBON UPTAKE ASTM D3860 / ISOTHERM

Wood Charcoal / Activated Carbon

Porous carbon media adsorbs emulsified motor oil hydrocarbons, fuel residues, and anionic surfactants (detergents) via hydrophobic surface interactions and micropore capture.

1
High-Surface-Area Carbon Granules Extensive microporous internal structure captures dissolved aromatic compounds and free surfactants.
2
Anionic Surfactant & Detergent Capture Attenuates commercial wash soap concentrations (median 13 mg/L) to break emulsified oil-water bonds.
Thickness 60 mm Bed
Particle Size 1.5–4.0 mm Granules
Specific Surface Area 400–800 m²/g
Oil Adsorption Cap. 14.2 mg/g Media
LAYER 05 · RESERVOIR & METAL EXCHANGE ETHIOPIAN RIFT VALLEY DEPOSIT

Vesicular Volcanic Pumice Sub-base

Locally sourced Ethiopian Great Rift Valley volcanic pumice provides a lightweight reservoir sub-base. Its honeycomb vesicular cavities host microbial biofilm and adsorb heavy metal ions (Pb, Zn).

1
Vesicular Volcanic Pumice Matrix High internal pore volume acts as a temporary hydraulic buffer during peak vehicle wash downpours.
2
Heavy Metal Cation Exchange Sites Natural alumino-silicate framework binds heavy metals from brake dust and exhaust (Pb = 0.53 mg/L, Zn = 1.98 mg/L).
Thickness 80 mm Sub-base
Particle Size 10–20 mm Fraction
Total Porosity 60%–75% Bulk Volume
Cation Exchange (CEC) 25–40 meq/100g
LAYER 06 · GRAVITY COLLECTION SYSTEM ASTM D1785 / SCHED 40

Perforated Underdrain & Pea Gravel

Embedded in a coarse pea gravel envelope (10–20 mm), the slotted collector pipe receives filtered effluent from the entire footprint and conveys it smoothly to the gravity oil separator without backwater pooling.

1
Perforated / Slotted PVC Collector Pipe 1.5 mm × 25 mm precision transverse slots provide maximum inflow area while excluding gravel intrusion.
2
10–20 mm Coarse Gravel Envelope Maintains high void space around pipe perforations to prevent local flow constriction.
Envelope Thickness 70 mm Depth
Pipe Diameter 50 mm (2 in) PVC
Conveyance Slope 1.5% Longitudinal Fall
Peak Flow Capacity >2.5 L/s Gravity
LAYER 07 · OIL-WATER SEPARATION & SENSORS API 421 / STOKES' LAW

Baffled Oil Separator & Reuse Tank

Two-compartment baffled unit engineered to Stokes' Law. Free oil droplets rise and collect behind the skimming weir, while clarified treated water flows beneath the underflow baffle into the reuse tank.

1
Baffled Stokes' Law Separator (API 421) Quiescent zone promotes buoyant oil rise for droplets down to 150 µm diameter.
2
Non-Potable Reuse Tank (SDG 6.3 & 6.4) Stores clarified effluent for vehicle pre-rinsing and wheel washing with optical turbidity verification.
Separator Dimensions 200 × 300 × 180 mm
Effluent Target <5 mg/L Oil Content
Sensor Telemetry Turbidity + Ultrasonic Head
Target Standard EEPA / WHO Non-Potable
