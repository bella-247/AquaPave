Profile

Role in AquaPave

Bezawit leads the chemical engineering work on the filter bed: choosing local media, setting layer depths, and testing how well each material removes oil, soap, and metals.

The role works with civil engineering at the slab interface and with electromechanical engineering on head loss and residence time, so the filter can be built inside the prototype.

Key deliverables

  • Filter media selection matrix and local-supplier notes.
  • Prepared and tested samples of sand, charcoal, pumice, and activated carbon.
  • Graded layer specification and geotextile interface details.
  • Oil, soap, and turbidity removal records from bench tests.

Weekly contributions

Weeks 1–3 were completed during the proposal phase. Weeks 4–12 are the planned work for this role.

Week 1: Team formation

Completed

Joined the team meeting and confirmed the chemical engineering role. Listed the lab equipment needed for filter and adsorption tests.

02

Week 2: Project selection

Completed

03

Week 3: Proposal and Gate 1

Completed

Wrote the filtration section of the proposal: media order, layer depths, and the removal mechanisms expected for each layer.

04

Week 4: Design and material planning

Planned

Set the media grading, layer depths, and jar-test plan. Costed filter materials with local suppliers.

05

Week 5: Procurement and pavement prep

Planned

Washed, dried, and sieved the sand, gravel, charcoal, and pumice. Prepared labelled samples for the bench tests.

06

Week 6: Filtration and mechanical build

Planned

Arranged the graded filter layers and checked the geotextile interface. Recorded layer thicknesses for the Gate 3 report.

07

Week 7: Electronics and software

Planned

Ran batch adsorption checks for oil and soap. Compared charcoal and pumice performance and noted the best mix.

08

Week 8: Prototype integration

Planned

Inspected the filter bed during integration and checked for bypass paths, media loss, and uneven layers.

09

Week 9: Initial testing

Planned

Sampled water before and after each filter layer. Measured turbidity and visual oil removal with the environmental team.

10

Week 10: Optimization

Planned

11

Week 11: Final testing and documentation
