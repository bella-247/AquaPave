Profile

Role in AquaPave

Helen leads the electromechanical build: the frame, watertight wash tray, slotted underdrain, baffled oil separator, reuse tank, and plumbing connections.

The role turns the chemical and architectural requirements into a buildable, leak-tested system, working closely with civil engineering at the slab interface.

Key deliverables

  • Structural frame and watertight wash tray.
  • Slotted PVC underdrain and collection pipe.
  • Baffled oil separator and reuse tank.
  • Leak-test and as-built mechanical records.
