Section 2.1 · Task allocation

Disciplinary roles and technical deliverables

Responsibilities are distributed across ten role slots covering seven engineering departments. Each discipline owns a defined set of technical deliverables that feed the integrated prototype.

Multidisciplinary task allocation across the ten Group 3 role slots.
Slot Engineering department Assigned responsibilities and technical deliverables
1 Civil Engineering Design the pervious concrete pavement mix, determine pavement layer thicknesses, prepare the concrete mixture, cast and cure the pervious and control slabs, and conduct ASTM infiltration and void-content tests.
2 Chemical Engineering Select and evaluate filter materials such as washed sand, wood charcoal, volcanic pumice, and activated carbon; assess their pollutant adsorption and removal capacity.
3 Environmental Engineering Investigate Addis Ababa garage wastewater characteristics, support wastewater sampling, formulate synthetic wash water, evaluate environmental impacts, and assess non-potable water reuse potential.
4 Architectural Engineering Prepare the overall wash-pad prototype layout, develop structural and visual 2D/3D drawings, optimize the 2%–5% slope geometry, and build the architectural scale model.
5 Electromechanical Engineering Design and fabricate the structural prototype frame, watertight washing tray, slotted drainage pipes, baffled oil separator, reuse collection tank, and mechanical connections.
6 Electrical and Computer Engineering (1) Select suitable water-level and turbidity sensors, design sensor signal-conditioning circuits, and develop the electrical or solar power supply arrangement.
7 Electrical and Computer Engineering (2) Interface the ESP32 microcontroller with the water-level, turbidity, and flow sensors, and execute hardware wiring and waterproofing integration.
8 Electrical and Computer Engineering (3) Test and calibrate the sensor system against reference standards, troubleshoot electrical connections, and verify accurate sensor readings.
9 Software Engineering (1) Develop the embedded ESP32 firmware for continuous sensor data acquisition, signal filtering, processing, and timestamped data logging.
10 Software Engineering (2) Develop the hydraulic clogging detection algorithm, automated maintenance alert system, and user monitoring interface, supporting end-to-end software integration.
Section 2.2 · Integration

Cross-disciplinary interfaces

Four primary interfaces keep dimensions, hydraulics, water-quality protocols, and electronics aligned across the prototype.

Interface 1

Pavement-to-filter coupling

Architectural Engineering defines the wash-pad footprint and 2%–5% slope; Civil Engineering ensures the cast slab meets void-content and strength requirements; Chemical Engineering specifies the geotextile and stratified media depths directly beneath the slab.

Interface 2

Filter-to-hydraulics coupling

Electromechanical Engineering fabricates the watertight tray, slotted PVC underdrain, baffled oil chamber, and reuse tank to match the head-loss and residence-time requirements specified by Chemical Engineering.

Interface 3

Water quality and benchmark protocol

Environmental Engineering establishes the synthetic wash-water formulation, stage-wise sampling schedule, and non-potable reuse limits used by Civil and Chemical Engineering to evaluate treatment efficiency and clogging recovery.

Interface 4

Smart monitoring integration

Electromechanical Engineering installs waterproof sensor ports; Electrical and Computer Engineering wires and calibrates the ESP32; Software Engineering implements the clogging detection algorithm and maintenance alert interface.
