Profile

Role in AquaPave

Khalid owns the civil engineering side of the wash pad: the pervious concrete mix, the slab and control specimens, and the tests that check how quickly water moves through the surface.

The role connects directly to the architectural layout and the filter bed below the slab. Khalid checks that the pavement layers, slope, and joint details match the drawings and still leave enough void space for water to pass.

Key deliverables

  • Pervious concrete mix design and trial proportions.
  • Cast pervious slab plus a dense control slab for comparison.
  • ASTM infiltration and void-content test records.
  • Curing, strength, and surface-finish notes for the final report.

Week by week

Weekly contributions

Weeks 1–3 were completed during the proposal phase. Weeks 4–12 are the planned work for this role.

01

Week 1: Team formation

Completed

Joined the first team meeting, confirmed the civil engineering role, and reviewed lab access and concrete safety rules. Set up a shared folder for mix designs and test records.

02

Week 2: Project selection

Completed

Compared a cast pervious slab with modular pavers for a small garage wash pad. Helped select AquaPave after reviewing buildability, cost, and local materials.

03

Week 3: Proposal and Gate 1

Completed

Prepared the pavement section, target void range (15–35%), and test plan for the proposal. Defined the dense control slab used as the benchmark.

04

Week 4: Design and material planning

Planned

Locked the layer thicknesses, mix proportions, and aggregate and cement quantities. Added the infiltration and void-content tests to the procurement plan.

05

Week 5: Procurement and pavement prep

Planned

Prepared the molds and cast the pervious slab and dense control slab. Started the seven-day wet cure and recorded mix and slump notes.

06

Week 6: Filtration and mechanical build

Planned

Checked the curing slabs, surface finish, and void structure. Confirmed the slab dimensions and drainage interface with the filter bed.

07

Week 7: Electronics and software

Planned

Reviewed sensor and pipe penetration points so the slab stays sealed. Prepared the infiltration test equipment for the integrated prototype.

08

Week 8: Prototype integration

Planned

Mounted and sealed the cured slab over the filter bed and checked the slope, edges, and perimeter seal. Supported the one-hour leak test.

09

Week 9: Initial testing

Planned

Ran baseline infiltration tests and compared runoff with the control slab. Logged drain times and marked any ponding.

10

Week 10: Optimization

Planned

Refined surface preparation and edge sealing where water collected. Repeated infiltration checks and recorded the improvement.

11

Week 11: Final testing and documentation

Planned

Completed triplicate infiltration and clogging-recovery tests. Prepared the pavement tables, photos, and notes for the final report.

12

Week 12: Presentation and submission

Planned

Supported the live demonstration and final submission, and answered questions about the pavement mix and test results.
