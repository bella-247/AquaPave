Profile

Role in AquaPave

Abel leads the embedded software for the monitoring system: the ESP32 firmware that samples the sensors, filters noisy readings, and writes timestamped data for the team.

The role works closely with electrical engineering on wiring and calibration, and with environmental engineering on which water-quality measurements matter.

Key deliverables

  • ESP32 firmware for sensor acquisition and timestamped logging.
  • Sampling and filtering settings for stable readings.
  • Data export used by the testing and analysis team.
  • Firmware notes and test evidence for the final report.
